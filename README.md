##  ElectroHub Offline Store Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

# Project Overview

The "ElectroHub Offline Store Sales Analysis" dashboard is an interactive "Power BI Business Intelligence" project designed to analyze offline retail sales performance across multiple product categories, cities, and promotional campaigns. The dashboard provides stakeholders with actionable insights into sales, profit, discounts, order volume, and product performance through dynamic visualizations and interactive filters. The project objectives include analyzing top/bottom-performing products, sales trends, profitability, discount effectiveness, city-wise sales distribution, and detailed order-level data.

# Project Objectives

1) Top/Bottom 5 product by Sales/Profit/Quantity Sold.
2) How do sales trends vary over time (daily, monthly, quarterly, annually) ?
3) Show relationship between sales & profit.
4) Compare sales/profit/quantity sold between any two periods selected by the user.
5) Average discount offered in each discount category.
6) Total number of orders.
7) Show Sales/Profit/Discount/Net Sales/All remaining fields for each order that could be
   filtered using visual filters. (Product/Date/Customer ID/Promotion Categories)
8) Show sales by different cities

# Key Insights

* Total Sales: ₹122M
* Total Profit: ₹12.2M
* Total Quantity Sold: 7.1K
* Total Orders: 3,510
* Apple iPhone 14 emerged as the highest-performing product by both sales and profit.
* Product performance varies significantly across categories and promotional campaigns, highlighting opportunities for inventory optimization and marketing decisions.
